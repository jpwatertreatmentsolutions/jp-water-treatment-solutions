Water Softener and Hard-Water Treatment

Borewell and other hard water contains calcium and magnesium that cause scale. We provide suitable water-softening solutions depending on your water quality and requirements. A water softener can reduce hardness and scale formation in pipes, heaters, boilers and appliances. It is a water treatment measure and not a medical treatment.
