Which water treatment services do you offer?

We offer STP manufacturing and installation, STP operation, manpower and training, AMC and maintenance, RO plants, water softener systems, ETP and industrial wastewater treatment, and industrial water treatment.

Do you manufacture and install STP plants?

Yes. We manufacture and install sewage treatment plants, including for newly constructed apartments. Depending on the project, this can include iron structures or civil tanks, motors, pumps, diffusers, pipelines and electrical and plumbing connections, followed by commissioning and handover.

Can you operate the plant or train our staff?

Where required, we can provide manpower to operate the plant, guide operators you appoint and train your staff. These services are agreed separately from installation.

What does an AMC include?

Our AMC provides scheduled visits, monthly or once every 15 days according to the agreed plan, with checks on pumps and tanks, sludge monitoring, odour-control inspection, plant servicing and inspection reports. Manpower, chemicals, spare parts, repairs and visit frequency depend on the agreed scope.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, and provide services across Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Bengaluru, Karnataka. Share your site location and we will discuss your requirement.

What is the difference between an STP and an ETP?

An STP treats sewage from buildings such as apartments, hotels and offices. An ETP treats wastewater produced by industrial processes. The right choice depends on where the wastewater comes from.

What information do I need for a quote?

Share the type of plant you need, your water source or wastewater type, approximate daily volume and site location. A water or effluent test report helps, but is not required to start the conversation.
