# JP Water Treatment Solutions: source notes (for the developer who edits the site)

The website is plain static HTML/CSS/JS. The four pages (plus privacy policy and 404) are generated by `build.py` so the header, footer, navigation and service content are written once.

## What lives where
- `build.py` : content and layout of every page. Service text is the `S` list near the top; FAQs are `FAQ` and `AFAQ`; business details (phone, address, email, social links, domain) are the constants at the top (`BASE`, `PHONE_D`, `ADDR`, `EMAIL`, `IG`, `FB`, `GBP`).
- `check.py` : link, title, canonical, alt-text and JSON-LD checks (run it from a folder that contains a copy of the deployed site named `out/`).
- The deployed folder (`jp-water-site/`) also contains `assets/style.css`, `assets/site.js` and `public/images/`. Those three are edited directly, not generated.

## To change page content
1. Edit `build.py`.
2. Run `python3 build.py` (Python 3.9+, no extra packages). It writes `out/index.html`, `out/services/index.html`, `out/about-us/index.html`, `out/contact-us/index.html`, `out/privacy-policy/index.html`, `out/404.html`, `out/sitemap.xml`, `out/robots.txt`.
3. Copy those files over the matching files in the deployed folder.
4. If you changed `assets/style.css` or `assets/site.js`, change `ASSET_V` at the top of `build.py` (for example to today's date) and rebuild, so visitors do not get a cached old file.

## Settings you will touch most
- `assets/site.js`, top of the file: `GA4_ID`, `GTM_ID`, `FORM_ENDPOINT`, `BUSINESS_EMAIL`, `MATH_CHECK`.
- Domain: `BASE` in `build.py`, or run `set-domain.sh` in the deployed folder.

## Notes
- Real photos are in `public/images/projects/` as `name-800.jpg/.webp` and `name-1536.jpg/.webp`. AI-generated illustrations remain only for AMC, Industrial Water Treatment and ETP-style placeholders listed in `IM` in `build.py`; replace them with real photos when available.
- The analytics cookie banner only appears once GA4 or GTM IDs are set.
