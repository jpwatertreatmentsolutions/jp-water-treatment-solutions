STP Operation, Manpower & Training

A treatment plant needs regular attention to work well. Where required, we provide manpower to operate treatment plants, guide the operators you appoint and train your staff. Operation, manpower and training are separate from plant installation and are agreed separately according to your needs.

Common problems and requirements

  • No trained person available to run the plant
  • Operators who need guidance on routine procedures
  • Staff who need training in basic plant care

Typical applications

  • Apartment and community STPs
  • Hotels, hospitals and institutions
  • Commercial buildings

What is included

  • We provide manpower to operate the plant
  • We guide operators appointed by the customer
  • We train the customer's staff when required

Scope, number of operators, duration and charges are agreed separately from the installation.

What to share for a quote

  • Type and capacity of the plant
  • Whether you need our operators, guidance for yours, or staff training
  • Working hours or shifts needed
  • Site location
